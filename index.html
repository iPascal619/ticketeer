Why Singapore GP Is Unmissable

The only night street race on the F1 calendar — where the city becomes the circuit and every corner is a spectacle.

Night Race Atmosphere

The world's only F1 night race transforms Singapore's skyline into a breathtaking backdrop. Watch cars blaze through floodlit streets as the city glows around you.

World-Class Entertainment

Beyond the racing — enjoy headline concerts, live performances, and entertainment zones throughout the race weekend. Previous acts include Eminem, Green Day, and The Weeknd.

Iconic Street Circuit

5.063km through the heart of Singapore — racing past Marina Bay Sands, the Merlion, and the Padang. The most visually stunning circuit on the calendar.

Culinary Experience

Singapore's legendary food scene meets F1 hospitality. From hawker-style street food inside the circuit to Michelin-star dining in the Paddock Club.

3-Day Festival

Your ticket gives you 3 full days of action — Friday practice, Saturday qualifying, Sunday race — plus full access to entertainment zones, food villages, and fan activities.

World-Class Destination

Combine your race weekend with everything Singapore offers — Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa Island, Orchard Road shopping, and one of Asia's most vibrant nightlife scenes.
